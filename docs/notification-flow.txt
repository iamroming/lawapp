WHATSAPP MESSAGES SENT:
========================

  TO LAWYER                          TO FIRM OWNER
  ─────────────────────              ─────────────────────
  ⚖️ Hearing Reminder                ⚖️ Hearing Alert — Firm Owner

  Dear [Lawyer],                     Dear [Owner],

  Case: [Number] - [Title]           A hearing is scheduled tomorrow
  Date: [Date]                       for your firm:
  Court: [Court]

                                     Case: [Number] - [Title]
  _CaseFiles_                        Lawyer: [Name]
                                     Date: [Date]
                                     Court: [Court]

                                     _CaseFiles_


NOTES:
======
  - Owner gets ONE message per firm (not per hearing)
  - Skip if owner IS the assigned lawyer (no duplicate)
  - Skip if owner has no phone number
  - Each message logged to whatsapp_logs table
