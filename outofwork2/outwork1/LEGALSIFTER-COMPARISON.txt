2. WHAT CAUSLY IS MISSING (Gaps from Legal Sifter)
================================================================================

HIGH PRIORITY (Can implement with existing AI infrastructure):

  1. CONTRACT REVIEW AI
     - Legal Sifter: Upload contract → AI identifies risky clauses,
       missing provisions, suggests improvements
     - Causly gap: AI only analyzes cases you DESCRIBE, not documents
     - How to build: Upload contract → send to LLM with clause-specific
       prompts → return risk analysis per section
     - Effort: 3-5 days

  2. DOCUMENT FULL-TEXT SEARCH
     - Legal Sifter: Search inside document contents
     - Causly gap: Only searches file names and titles
     - How to build: Extract text on upload (PDF/docx parsing) → store
       in a text column → use Supabase full-text search
     - Effort: 2-3 days

  3. MISSING PROVISION DETECTION
     - Legal Sifter: Checks if contract has required clauses for its type
     - Causly gap: No contract validation
     - How to build: Define required clauses per contract type (e.g.,
       "rental agreement must have: rent amount, notice period, deposit")
       → AI checks presence/absence
     - Effort: 2-3 days

  4. CLAUSE RISK SCORING
     - Legal Sifter: Each clause gets a risk score
     - Causly gap: Only overall case strength score
     - How to build: Parse contract into sections → score each section
       → highlight high-risk clauses
     - Effort: 2-3 days

MEDIUM PRIORITY (Requires new infrastructure):

  5. CONTRACT TEMPLATES WITH VARIABLES
     - Legal Sifter: Dynamic templates with placeholders
     - Causly gap: Static templates only
     - How to build: Template engine with {{party_name}}, {{date}}, etc.
     - Effort: 2-3 days

  6. APPROVAL WORKFLOWS
     - Legal Sifter: Route contracts through approval chains
     - Causly gap: No approval system
     - How to build: Define workflow states (draft → review → approved)
       → assign reviewers → track status
     - Effort: 3-4 days

  7. OBLIGATION TRACKING
     - Legal Sifter: Track post-signature obligations and deadlines
     - Causly gap: Only has calendar reminders
     - How to build: Extract obligations from contracts → create tasks
       → link to reminders
     - Effort: 3-4 days

  8. TIME TRACKING FOR BILLING
     - Legal Sifter: Track billable hours per contract review
     - Causly gap: No time tracking module
     - How to build: Timer + manual entries → linked to cases/clients
       → feed into invoices
     - Effort: See TIME-MANAGEMENT-MODULE-PLAN.txt

LOW PRIORITY (Nice to have):

  9. WORD PLUGIN / ADD-IN
     - Legal Sifter: Works inside Microsoft Word
     - Causly gap: Standalone web app only
     - How to build: Office.js add-in or browser extension
     - Effort: 5-7 days

 10. E-SIGNATURE INTEGRATION
     - Legal Sifter: DocuSign / Adobe Sign integration
     - Causly gap: No e-signature
     - How to build: Integrate with Zoho Sign or RocketSign (Indian)
     - Effort: 3-5 days

 11. OCR (OPTICAL CHARACTER RECOGNITION)
     - Legal Sifter: Extract text from scanned documents
     - Causly gap: Stores images but can't read them
     - How to build: Google Vision API or Tesseract on upload
     - Effort: 2-3 days

 12. CRM / HUBSPOT INTEGRATION
     - Legal Sifter: Sync with CRM systems
     - Causly gap: Standalone client management
     - How to build: API integrations with popular CRMs
     - Effort: 3-5 days per integration

3. WHAT CAUSLY HAS THAT LEGAL SIFTER DOESN'T
================================================================================

  These are Causly's competitive advantages — things Legal Sifter cannot do:

  1. FULL PRACTICE MANAGEMENT
     - Cases, clients, billing, calendar, team — all in one
     - Legal Sifter is ONLY contract review, not a practice tool

  2. eCOURTS INTEGRATION
     - Track live cases on Indian courts
     - Unique to Indian legal market — no global competitor has this

  3. INDIAN BARE ACTS REFERENCE
     - Searchable Indian laws database
     - Unique to Indian legal market

  4. INDIAN LEGAL CALCULATORS
     - Stamp duty, court fees, limitation periods
     - Unique to Indian legal market

  5. CLIENT PORTAL
     - Clients can log in and view their cases
     - Legal Sifter has no client-facing portal

  6. TEAM MANAGEMENT
     - Employee roles, permissions, performance tracking
     - Legal Sifter is individual-seat focused

  7. BILLING & INVOICING
     - Generate invoices, collect payments via Razorpay
     - Legal Sifter has no billing system

  8. SUBSCRIPTION BUSINESS MODEL
     - Tiered plans (Free/Solo/Professional/Enterprise)
     - Legal Sifter uses per-seat pricing ($29/user/month)

  9. AI CASE ANALYSIS
     - Analyze case strength, strategy, precedents
     - Legal Sifter analyzes CONTRACTS, not cases

 10. MESSAGES / INTERNAL COMMUNICATION
     - Team messaging system
     - Legal Sifter has no messaging

4. RECOMMENDATION — BUILD vs SKIP
================================================================================

  BUILD (High ROI, fits Causly's Indian market):
  ✓ Contract Review AI — use existing AI infrastructure
  ✓ Document full-text search — low effort, high value
  ✓ Missing provision detection — differentiator for Indian firms
  ✓ Time tracking — already planned, essential for billing
  ✓ Obligation tracking — extends existing reminders

  SKIP (Not relevant for Indian legal market):
  ✗ Microsoft Word plugin — Indian lawyers use web-based tools
  ✗ HubSpot/CRM integration — Indian firms don't use these
  ✗ DocuSign integration — Indian firms use physical signatures
  ✗ Zapier integration — not popular in Indian legal market

  DEFER (Build later if demand exists):
  △ Approval workflows — needed for larger firms
  △ OCR — useful but not urgent
  △ E-signature — wait for Indian e-sign adoption
