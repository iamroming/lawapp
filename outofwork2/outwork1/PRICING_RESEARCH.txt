PART 4: KEY INSIGHTS FROM COMPETITORS
============================================================

1. PER-USER PRICING IS STANDARD
   - Most Indian competitors use per-seat/per-user pricing
   - Allows revenue to scale with firm growth
   - Clio, LawSathi, Bharat.Law all use this model

2. 3 PLANS IS THE SWEET SPOT
   - Solo/Free → Professional → Enterprise
   - Too many plans causes decision paralysis
   - LegalDoc360, Vakilbase, Bharat.Law all use 3-4 tiers

3. ANNUAL BILLING = 15-25% DISCOUNT
   - Universal across all competitors
   - Improves cash flow and reduces churn
   - Bharat.Law: ~33% discount annually

4. FREE TIER IS LIGHTWEIGHT
   - 5-10 cases max, limited users
   - Acts as lead generation, not a real plan
   - Most free tiers are 1 user only

5. AI IS A PREMIUM FEATURE
   - Most charge extra for AI or limit credits
   - Barrister24: ₹75/draft overage
   - LawSathi: ₹1,299/mo for AI features
   - AI should be a differentiator, not free

6. INDIAN PRICE SENSITIVITY
   - Sweet spot: ₹500-2,000/mo for small firms
   - Professional tier: ₹1,000-3,000/mo
   - Enterprise starts at ₹3,000+/mo

7. STORAGE IS A REVENUE STREAM
   - Extra storage bundles: ₹200/mo for 50GB
   - Most plans cap at 5-25 GB on base tier

8. 14-DAY FREE TRIAL IS STANDARD
   - LawSathi, Vakilbase, Bharat.Law all offer 14 days
   - No credit card required
