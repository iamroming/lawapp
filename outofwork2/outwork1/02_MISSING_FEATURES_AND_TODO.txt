--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A1. CRITICAL - INDIA MARKET MUST-HAVES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[1] e-COURT INTEGRATION
    Why   : Indian courts have e-filing and e-court status systems. Direct
            integration is THE biggest differentiator for Indian lawyers.
    What   : Fetch case status, next hearing dates, orders/judgments directly
            from eCourts.gov.in API.
    Who    : Legistify, Provakil already do this.
    Impact : Without this, you're just another generic CRM.

[2] LIMITATION PERIOD CALCULATOR
    Why   : Missing a filing deadline (limitation period) can cost a case.
            Lawyers need to calculate appeal (30/60/90 days), revision
            (90 days), review (30 days) deadlines constantly.
    What   : Auto-calculate based on case type, section, and state.
    Who    : ManageMyLawsuits, Indian Kanoon.
    Impact : This single feature saves lawyers from malpractice.

[3] COURT FEE / STAMP DUTY CALCULATOR
    Why   : Court fees and stamp duty vary by state, case type, and claim
            amount. Manual calculation is error-prone.
    What   : State-wise calculator with automatic computation.
    Who    : Indian-specific legal tools.
    Impact : Essential for filing any case.

[4] GST-COMPLIANT INVOICES
    Why   : Indian law requires GST-compliant invoices. Current invoices
            don't have GSTIN, HSN/SAC codes, state codes, or proper format.
    What   : Full GST invoice format with all required fields.
    Who    : Every billing software in India.
    Impact : Cannot legally bill clients without this.

[5] WHATSAPP/SMS NOTIFICATIONS
    Why   : Indian lawyers live on WhatsApp. Hearing reminders, payment
            due alerts, and case updates via WhatsApp are essential.
    What   : WhatsApp Business API integration for automated reminders.
    Who    : ManageMyLawsuits, most Indian legal apps.
    Impact : Lawyers will not check email regularly. WhatsApp is how they work.

[6] MULTI-LANGUAGE SUPPORT (HINDI + ENGLISH)
    Why   : Many Indian lawyers (especially outside metros) prefer Hindi
            or regional languages. Documents often need to be in Hindi.
    What   : Hindi UI at minimum. Hindi document templates.
    Who    : Provakil, Legistify support multiple Indian languages.
    Impact : Limits adoption to English-speaking urban lawyers only.

[7] VAKALATNAMA & CASE DIARY TEMPLATES
    Why   : Vakalatnama is the authority letter from client to lawyer.
            Case diary is mandatory in many courts.
    What   : Digital vakalatnama template, case diary management.
    Who    : Indian-specific legal apps.
    Impact : Paper-based processes that need digitization.

[8] e-FILING INTEGRATION
    Why   : Indian courts increasingly require e-filing. Direct integration
            saves hours of manual work.
    What   : Direct filing with court portals from within the app.
    Who    : Provakil, MCA21 integration tools.
    Impact : Future-proofing for mandatory e-filing.

[9] INDIAN COURT HIERARCHY IN DROPDOWNS
    Why   : Current dropdowns have generic court names. Indian court system
            is: Supreme Court → High Court → District → Sessions → Magistrate.
    What   : Proper Indian court hierarchy with state-wise mapping.
    Who    : All Indian legal apps.
    Impact : Users have to type court names manually, leading to inconsistency.

[10] STATE-WISE JURISDICTION MAPPING
     Why   : Different states have different courts, fees, and procedures.
     What   : Auto-map court based on state/district selection.
     Who   : Indian-specific apps.
     Impact : Current app has generic court list without state context.

A2. IMPORTANT - CORE PRODUCT GAPS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[11] CLIENT PORTAL
     Why   : Clients want to see their case status without calling the
             lawyer. This reduces back-and-forth communication.
     What   : Separate client login, view case status, download documents,
             view and pay invoices.
     Who    : Clio (Clio for Clients), MyCase, PracticePanther.
     Impact : Major differentiator. Lawyers can offer better client service.

[12] CONFLICT OF INTEREST CHECK
     Why   : Lawyers MUST check if a new client conflicts with existing
             clients before taking the case. Ethical obligation.
     What   : Auto-check new client name/party against existing database.
     Who    : Clio, PracticePanther, most enterprise legal tools.
     Impact : Legal liability if not checked.

[13] TASK MANAGEMENT PER CASE
     Why   : Cases have multiple tasks (draft petition, file response,
             prepare witness, etc.) that need tracking.
     What   : Per-case tasks with assignees, deadlines, status, reminders.
     Who    : Clio, MyCase, PracticePanther.
     Impact : Lawyers juggle dozens of tasks across cases. Need tracking.

[14] EMAIL INTEGRATION
     Why   : Lawyers communicate with clients and courts via email.
             Emails related to cases should be linked to the case.
     What   : Auto-link emails to cases, send from within app, email templates.
     Who    : Clio, PracticePanther.
     Impact : Email threads get lost. Case context is scattered.

[15] DOCUMENT TEMPLATES
     Why   : Lawyers draft the same types of documents repeatedly.
             Templates save hours of work.
     What   : Pre-built templates for petition, notice, agreement, affidavit,
             legal notice, reply, application, etc.
     Who    : Clio Draft, PracticePanther, Indian legal apps.
     Impact : Drastic time savings for routine documents.

[16] CLAUSE LIBRARY
     Why   : Contract lawyers need common clauses readily available.
     What   : Searchable library of common contract clauses.
     Who    : Contract management tools.
     Impact : Speeds up contract drafting significantly.

[17] LEGAL RESEARCH INTEGRATION
     Why   : Lawyers need to research case law. Quick access saves time.
     What   : Direct links to Indian Kanoon, LiveLaw, SCC Online from
             within case records.
     Who    : Provakil, Legistify.
     Impact : Keeps lawyers in the app instead of switching tools.

[18] TRUST ACCOUNTING
     Why   : Many jurisdictions require client trust accounts (IOLTA).
             Mishandling trust funds is a serious ethical violation.
     What   : Client trust account management with proper accounting.
     Who    : Clio, MyCase (LawPay).
     Impact : Required for compliance in many jurisdictions.

[19] E-SIGNATURE
     Why   : Documents need signatures. Digital signatures save time.
     What   : E-signature support for documents within the app.
     Who    : Clio (DocuSign integration), PracticePanther.
     Impact : Eliminates need for physical document signing.

[20] LEAD MANAGEMENT
     Why   : Law firms need to track potential clients from first contact
             through engagement.
     What   : Lead capture, follow-up tracking, conversion analytics.
     Who    : Clio Grow, Lawmatics.
     Impact : Firms lose potential clients without lead tracking.

[21] PAYMENT GATEWAY (RAZORPAY/UPI)
     Why   : Clients want to pay online. UPI is dominant in India.
     What   : Razorpay integration for UPI, cards, net banking payments.
     Who    : MyCase (LawPay), Clio Payments.
     Impact : Faster payment collection, better cash flow.

[22] PROPER AUDIT TRAIL
     Why   : Legal data requires accountability. Who changed what and when?
     What   : Track all changes with before/after values, timestamp, user.
     Who    : Clio, PracticePanther, all enterprise tools.
     Impact : Compliance requirement for many firms.

[23] DATA EXPORT (EXCEL/CSV)
     Why   : Lawyers need to export data for reports, external analysis.
     What   : Export cases, clients, invoices to Excel/CSV.
     Who    : All competitors.
     Impact : Users feel locked in without export capability.

[24] BULK OPERATIONS
     Why   : Managing one case at a time is slow.
     What   : Select multiple cases, send bulk updates, bulk invoice.
     Who    : PracticePanther, enterprise tools.
     Impact   : Time savings for firms with many cases.

[25] NOTES WITH @MENTIONS
     Why   : Team members need to collaborate on cases.
     What   : Tag team members in case notes, notifications on mention.
     Who    : Clio, collaborative tools.
     Impact : Improves team communication.

[26] VOICE NOTES
     Why   : Lawyers dictate notes during/after court visits.
     What   : Record voice notes, auto-transcribe, attach to case.
     Who    : MyCase.
     Impact : Mobile-first feature for lawyers on the go.

[27] OCR FOR DOCUMENTS
     Why   : Many legal documents are scanned PDFs. Search requires text.
     What   : Extract text from scanned judgments/orders.
     Who    : Provakil.
     Impact : Makes scanned documents searchable.

[28] AI CASE SUMMARY
     Why   : Judgments are often 50+ pages. Quick summaries save time.
     What   : Auto-summarize judgments, extract key dates and orders.
     Who    : Emerging feature in legal tech.
     Impact : Major time saver for research.
