ROLE DESCRIPTIONS:
  Owner           - Firm owner, full access to everything including admin panel
  Partner         - Senior partner, same access as owner (minus account-level settings)
  Senior Associate- Experienced lawyer, access to reports, AI tools, billing
  Associate       - Mid-level lawyer, access to AI drafting, consultations, intake forms
  Junior Associate- Junior lawyer, access to AI assistant, chat, basic case tools
  Paralegal       - Support staff, access to research, documents, expenses, tasks
  Intern          - Trainee, limited to cases, calendar, tasks, timesheets, reminders
  Office Admin    - Administrative staff, access to billing, documents, clients, messages
