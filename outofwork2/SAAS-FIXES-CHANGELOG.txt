9. CSRF ON ALL STATE-CHANGING ROUTES
-------------------------------------
Problem: CSRF protection only covered 2 routes (/subscriptions/change
         and /subscriptions/verify). 60+ other POST/PUT/DELETE routes
         had no CSRF protection.

Files Modified:
  - src/middleware.ts
    Changed: CSRF validation now applies to ALL POST, PUT, DELETE
    requests on /api/* routes. Skips:
    - /api/subscriptions/webhook (uses Razorpay signature)

    - /api/auth/callback (OAuth callback)
