Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about LawXP.

Is LawXP built for the Indian legal system?
Absolutely. LawXP is built specifically for the Indian legal system. We integrate with eCourts for real-time case tracking, support cause list syncing, and understand the nuances of Indian court procedures across all High Courts and District Courts.
Is there a free trial?
Yes! Every plan comes with a 14-day free trial. No credit card required. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel anytime.
Can I import my existing cases?
Yes. You can bulk import cases from Excel/CSV, or add them one by one. Our team can help with migration for Enterprise plans.
Is my data secure?
Your data is encrypted at rest and in transit. We use Supabase (PostgreSQL) with row-level security. Your firm's data is completely isolated from other firms. We never access or share your data.
How does the AI assistant work?
LawXP includes an AI assistant trained on Indian legal knowledge. It can draft legal documents, summarize case law, answer legal questions, and help with research — all within the platform.
