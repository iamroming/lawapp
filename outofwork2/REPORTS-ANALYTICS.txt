CAUSLY - REPORTS & DATA ANALYTICS
=====================================

=========================================
PART 1: CURRENT REPORTS (What Exists)
=========================================

--- REPORTS HUB (/reports) ---
Access: Owner, Partner, Senior Associate
Shows:
  - Total Cases, Active Cases, Total Revenue, Upcoming Hearings (KPI cards)
  - Cases by Status (horizontal bar chart)
  - Monthly Revenue (horizontal bar chart)
  - Date range filter (This Month, Last 3 Months, This Year, Custom)
  - Export: PDF (print) + CSV

--- CASE STATISTICS (/reports/cases) ---
Access: Owner, Partner, Senior Associate
Shows:
  - Total Cases, Win Rate %, Avg Duration (days), Resolved Cases
  - Cases by Status (SVG donut chart)
  - Win/Loss Ratio (SVG donut chart)
  - Cases by Type (horizontal bar)
  - Cases by Court (horizontal bar)

--- REVENUE REPORT (/reports/revenue) ---
Access: Owner, Partner, Senior Associate
Shows:
  - Total Revenue, Outstanding, GST Collected, Pending Invoices
  - Monthly Revenue Trend (vertical bar chart)
  - Revenue by Client (top 10, horizontal bar)
  - Revenue by Case Type (horizontal bar)
  - GST Summary (CGST 9% + SGST 9%)

--- CLIENT REPORT (/reports/clients) ---
Access: Owner, Partner, Senior Associate
Shows:
  - Total Clients, Active Clients, New This Month, With Phone
  - Client Growth by Month (vertical bar chart)
  - Clients by Type (horizontal bar)
  - Top 10 Clients by Cases (horizontal bar)

--- PERFORMANCE METRICS (/reports/performance) ---
Access: Owner, Partner, Senior Associate
Shows:
  - Win Rate %, Avg Case Duration, Total Resolved, Active Lawyers
  - Performance by Lawyer (table: cases, won, lost, settled, win rate, revenue)
  - Monthly Case Resolution (vertical bar chart)
  - Cases by Priority (horizontal bar)

--- ADMIN PANEL (/admin) ---
Access: Owner, Partner only
Shows:
  - Total Employees, Total Clients, Active Cases, Total Revenue
  - Employee Leaderboard (ranked by cases handled)
  - Role Distribution
  - Salary Management, Profit Sharing

--- EMPLOYEE PERFORMANCE (/admin/performance) ---
Access: Owner, Partner only
Shows:
  - Total Employees, Clients Acquired, Cases Handled, Cases Won
  - Full leaderboard table with ranking

--- ACTIVITY LOGS (/admin/activity) ---
Access: Owner, Partner only
Shows:
  - Total Events, Events Today, Active Users Today
  - Searchable activity feed with filtering

--- BILLING REPORTS ---
Access: Owner, Partner, Senior Associate, Associate, Office Admin
Shows:
  - Outstanding Payments (aging buckets: 0-30, 31-60, 61-90, 90+ days)
  - Collections Workflow (reminder -> final notice -> legal notice -> recovery)
  - Invoice list with payment status

--- EXPENSES (/expenses) ---
Access: Owner, Partner, Senior Associate, Associate, Junior Associate, Paralegal
Shows:
  - Total Expenses, Billable, Unbilled, Non-Billable
  - Filterable expense list

--- TIMESHEETS (/timesheets) ---
Access: All roles
Shows:
  - Total Hours, Billable Hours, Entries count
  - Live timer for real-time tracking
  - Timesheet list with case links

PART 3: WHAT'S MISSING (Data Science Opportunities)
=========================================

--- A. CASE ANALYTICS ---

1. CASE DURATION BY TYPE
   "How long do criminal vs civil vs family cases take?"
   Data: cases.created_at, cases.status changes, cases.filing_date
   Chart: Box plot or bar chart showing avg duration per case type

2. WIN RATE BY COURT
   "Which courts have higher success rates?"
   Data: cases.court, cases.status (won/lost/settled)
   Chart: Grouped bar chart (won/lost per court)

3. WIN RATE BY JUDGE
   "Which judges rule favorably for our clients?"
   Data: cases.judge_name, cases.status
   Chart: Horizontal bar chart (win rate per judge)

4. CASE TIMELINE VISUALIZATION
   "Show the lifecycle of a case from filing to resolution"
   Data: cases.status_history (can be derived from workflow_events)
   Chart: Gantt-style timeline

5. CASE RE-OPEN RATE
   "How often are closed cases reopened?"
   Data: cases.status changes (closed -> active)
   Chart: Percentage card + trend line

6. SETTLEMENT VS TRIAL
   "What percentage settle vs go to trial?"
   Data: cases.status (settled vs won/lost)
   Chart: Pie chart + comparison

--- B. BILLING & FINANCIAL ANALYTICS ---

7. REVENUE PER LAWYER OVER TIME
   "Which lawyers bring in the most revenue month over month?"
   Data: cases.assigned_to, payments.received_at, payments.amount
   Chart: Stacked area chart (revenue by lawyer by month)

8. AVERAGE PAYMENT TIME
   "How long do clients take to pay?"
   Data: invoices.created_at, payments.received_at
   Chart: Histogram (days to pay) + average card

9. PAYMENT METHOD BREAKDOWN
   "Razorpay vs bank transfer vs cash?"
   Data: payments.payment_method
   Chart: Pie chart

10. REVENUE FORECASTING
    "Predict next month's revenue based on historical data"
    Data: Monthly revenue for last 12 months
    Chart: Line chart with forecast cone (linear regression)

11. PROFIT MARGIN BY CASE TYPE
    "Which case types are most profitable?"
    Data: cases.total_fee, expenses.amount (linked to case)
    Chart: Grouped bar chart (revenue vs expenses per type)

12. EXPENSE-TO-REVENUE RATIO
    "How much do we spend vs earn?"
    Data: expenses.total, revenue.total by month
    Chart: Dual-axis line chart

13. COUPON/DISCOUNT IMPACT
    "How much revenue do discounts cost?"
    Data: coupons, coupon_uses, invoices
    Chart: Summary card + trend

--- C. TEAM PERFORMANCE ANALYTICS ---

14. TIME UTILIZATION BY LAWYER
    "Billable vs non-billable hours per team member"
    Data: timesheets.hours, timesheets.billable
    Chart: Stacked bar chart (billable vs non-billable per person)

15. BILLABLE RATE EFFECTIVENESS
    "Revenue per billable hour per lawyer"
    Data: cases.total_fee, timesheets.hours, cases.assigned_to
    Chart: Bar chart (revenue/hour per lawyer)

16. CASE LOAD BALANCE
    "Are cases evenly distributed across the team?"
    Data: cases.assigned_to, count per person
    Chart: Bar chart + standard deviation indicator

17. TASK COMPLETION RATES
    "Who completes tasks on time?"
    Data: tasks.assigned_to, tasks.due_date, tasks.completed_at
    Chart: Completion rate % per person + trend

18. CONSULTATION-TO-CLIENT CONVERSION
    "What % of consultations become paying clients?"
    Data: consultations -> clients (linked)
    Chart: Funnel chart (consultation -> intake -> client)

--- D. CLIENT ANALYTICS ---

19. CLIENT LIFETIME VALUE (LTV)
    "Total revenue per client over all time"
    Data: clients.id, sum(payments.amount) per client
    Chart: Top 20 clients by LTV + average LTV card

20. CLIENT RETENTION/CHURN
    "Are clients returning for more cases?"
    Data: clients with multiple cases vs single case
    Chart: Retention rate % + cohort analysis

21. CLIENT ACQUISITION TREND
    "How many new clients per month over time?"
    Data: clients.created_at by month
    Chart: Line chart with trend

22. INTAKE-TO-CLIENT CONVERSION
    "What % of intake form submissions become clients?"
    Data: intake_submissions -> clients
    Chart: Funnel (submitted -> contacted -> onboarded)

--- E. HEARING & COURT ANALYTICS ---

23. HEARING FREQUENCY BY COURT
    "Which courts have the most hearings?"
    Data: hearings + cases.court
    Chart: Bar chart

24. ADJOURNMENT RATE
    "How often are hearings postponed?"
    Data: hearings (created vs completed vs adjourned)
    Chart: Adjournment % card + trend

25. COURT UTILIZATION HEATMAP
    "Which courts are busiest on which days?"
    Data: hearings.hearing_date (day of week)
    Chart: Heatmap (day x court)

26. AVERAGE HEARINGS PER CASE
    "How many hearings does a typical case take?"
    Data: count(hearings) per case
    Chart: Distribution histogram

--- F. PLATFORM ANALYTICS (SaaS Admin) ---

27. MRR/ARR TRACKING
    "Monthly and Annual Recurring Revenue"
    Data: user_subscriptions, subscription_plans
    Chart: MRR line chart + ARR card

28. CHURN RATE
    "How many subscribers cancel each month?"
    Data: user_subscriptions (cancelled status)
    Chart: Churn % trend line

29. TRIAL-TO-PAID CONVERSION
    "What % of trial users convert to paid?"
    Data: user_subscriptions (trialing -> active)
    Chart: Conversion funnel

30. COHORT RETENTION
    "Do users who sign up in January stick around?"
    Data: user_subscriptions by signup month
    Chart: Cohort retention heatmap

PART 5: TECHNICAL NOTES
=========================================

CHARTING LIBRARY OPTIONS:
  - Recharts (React-native, simple API, recommended)
  - Chart.js (lightweight, many chart types)
  - Nivo (built on D3, beautiful but heavy)
  - Tremor (Tailwind-native dashboard components)

DATA PIPELINE OPTIONS:
  - Keep current: Client-side Supabase queries (simple, real-time)
  - Upgrade: Server-side API routes with caching (faster, more secure)
  - Advanced: Background workers + materialized views (for heavy analytics)

EXPORT OPTIONS:
  - Current: CSV export (basic)
  - Add: PDF export with charts (using jsPDF + html2canvas)
  - Add: Excel export (using xlsx library)
  - Add: Scheduled email reports (weekly/monthly)
