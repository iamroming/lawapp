======================================================================
    MARKETING PLAN — CASEFILES (INDIAN LEGAL MARKET)
======================================================================

======================================================================
EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
======================================================================
Target: 1.5 million+ lawyers in India
Goal: 10,000 users in 12 months, 50,000 in 24 months
Strategy: Community-first, word-of-mouth, content marketing
Budget: ₹5-10 lakhs/month (lean startup approach)


======================================================================
PHASE 1: FOUNDATION (Months 1-3)
======================================================================

--- Content Marketing ---

1. Hindi + English Blog
   - "How to manage cases efficiently in 2026"
   - "GST invoicing guide for lawyers"
   - "eCourts integration: Complete guide"
   - "Client management tips for small firms"
   - Publish 3 posts/week (2 Hindi, 1 English)
   - SEO target: "case management software India", "legal billing India"

2. YouTube Channel
   - "CaseFiles Demo" — 5-minute product walkthrough
   - "How to" tutorials in Hindi
   - "Law practice management tips"
   - Target: 100 videos in 6 months
   - Hindi voiceover with English subtitles

3. LinkedIn Content
   - Founder's personal brand: legal tech thought leadership
   - Daily posts about legal tech, practice management
   - Engage in Indian lawyer groups
   - Target: 5000 followers in 6 months

--- Community Building ---

4. WhatsApp Groups
   - Create "Indian Legal Tech" WhatsApp group
   - Share tips, not just product updates
   - Target: 50 groups, 5000 members in 6 months
   - Regional groups: Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi

5. Bar Association Partnerships
   - Partner with Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore bar associations
   - Free workshops on "Technology for Lawyers"
   - Sponsor bar association events
   - Offer 3-month free trial to members

6. Legal Community Platforms
   - Active on Lawctopus, Bar & Bench, LiveLaw
   - Answer questions, share expertise
   - Guest posts on legal blogs

--- Referral Program ---

7. "Refer a Friend" Program
   - Refer 1 friend → 1 month free
   - Refer 3 friends → 3 months free
   - Refer 10 friends → 1 year free
   - Unique referral link per user
   - Track via referral_codes table

8. "Law Firm Champion" Program
   - 10+ users from same firm → free Firm plan for 6 months
   - Designate firm champion who gets premium support
   - Case study published about their firm


======================================================================
PHASE 2: GROWTH (Months 4-8)
======================================================================

--- Paid Acquisition ---

9. Google Ads
   - Target: "case management software India", "legal billing software"
   - Budget: ₹50,000/month
   - Expected CPC: ₹20-50
   - Landing page: /pricing with demo video

10. Facebook/Instagram Ads
    - Target: Lawyers, advocates, legal professionals
    - Age: 25-55, India
    - Interests: Law, legal practice, courtroom
    - Budget: ₹30,000/month
    - Creative: Before/After (manual vs CaseFiles)

11. LinkedIn Ads
    - Target: Law firm partners, managing partners
    - Job titles: Advocate, Lawyer, Partner, Associate
    - Budget: ₹20,000/month
    - Creative: ROI calculator (time saved = money saved)

--- Strategic Partnerships ---

12. Court Complex Installations
    - Partner with court complexes for free kiosks
    - Lawyers can try CaseFiles at court
    - QR code → instant signup
    - Target: 10 court complexes in 6 months

13. Legal Education Institutes
    - Partner with NLSIU, NALSAR, NLUD, etc.
    - Free access for law students
    - Students become users after graduation
    - Guest lectures on "Legal Technology"

14. CA/CS Firm Partnerships
    - Cross-sell to chartered accountants
    - Shared client management needs
    - Integration with Tally/other accounting tools

--- Product-Led Growth ---

15. Freemium Tier
    - Free plan: 3 cases, 1 user, 200MB
    - Enough to experience value
    - Upgrade prompt when limit hit
    - "Upgrade for more" email sequence

16. In-App Virality
    - Share case details via WhatsApp → "Powered by CaseFiles"
    - Client portal login page → "Your lawyer uses CaseFiles"
    - Invoice PDF footer → "Generated with CaseFiles"
    - Document watermark → "CaseFiles"

17. Templates & Tools
    - Free legal document templates (Vakalatnama, Legal Notice)
    - Free legal calculators (Stamp Duty, Limitation Period)
    - These rank on Google → drive traffic → convert to users


======================================================================
PHASE 3: SCALE (Months 9-12)
======================================================================

--- Regional Expansion ---

18. Regional Language Support
    - Hindi (done), Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Bengali, Gujarati
    - Regional content marketing
    - Regional WhatsApp groups
    - Regional customer support

19. State-Specific Features
    - State-wise stamp duty calculator
    - State-wise court fee calculator
    - State-specific procedural rules
    - Local court integrations

--- Enterprise Sales ---

20. Law Firm Enterprise Sales
    - Direct sales team for 50+ user firms
    - Custom onboarding and training
    - SLA guarantees
    - SSO/SAML integration
    - Target: 10 enterprise deals in 12 months

21. Legal Departments
    - Target: Corporate legal departments
    - Features: matter management, compliance tracking
    - Integration with corporate workflows

--- Events & Conferences ---

22. Legal Tech Conferences
    - Sponsor/attend: India Law Forum, Legal Tech Summit
    - Host: "CaseFiles Legal Tech Meetup" quarterly
    - Target: 500 attendees per event

23. Webinars
    - Weekly webinars on practice management
    - Guest speakers: senior advocates, judges
    - Record and publish on YouTube
    - Target: 1000 registrants per webinar


======================================================================
PHASE 4: RETENTION (Ongoing)
======================================================================

--- Customer Success ---

24. Onboarding Sequence
    - Day 1: Welcome email + setup guide
    - Day 3: "How to create your first case" video
    - Day 7: Check-in email + tips
    - Day 14: "You're doing great!" milestone
    - Day 30: Feedback survey + upgrade prompt

25. Customer Support
    - WhatsApp support (Indian users prefer WhatsApp)
    - Hindi + English support
    - Response time: < 2 hours
    - Knowledge base in Hindi + English

26. Community Events
    - Monthly "Lawyer Success Stories"
    - Quarterly "CaseFiles User Meetup"
    - Annual "CaseFiles Conference"
    - User-generated content contests

--- Retention Tactics ---

27. Usage-Based Emails
    - Low usage: "We miss you! Here's what's new"
    - High usage: "You're a power user! Upgrade for more"
    - Limit approaching: "You're almost at your limit"
    - Feature discovery: "Did you know you can..."

28. Loyalty Program
    - 1 year: Free upgrade to next plan
    - 2 years: Priority support
    - 3 years: Free add-ons
    - 5 years: Lifetime discount

29. Annual Plan Incentive
    - 2 months free on annual billing
    - Exclusive features for annual subscribers
    - Early access to new features

======================================================================
COMPETITIVE ADVANTAGES
======================================================================

1. Hindi-first: Only legal SaaS with full Hindi support
2. eCourts integration: Direct CNR tracking
3. WhatsApp-native: Share via WhatsApp (Indian lawyers' #1 tool)
4. Indian court system: Stamp duty, limitation, court fees
5. Affordable: ₹299/month (cheaper than a court visit)
6. Mobile-first: Works on phone (lawyers are always on the move)
7. Client portal: Clients can track their cases
8. AI tools: Legal research, document drafting
9. Multi-tenant: Full firm data isolation
10. Bar association partnerships: Trust factor
