========================================
FREE TRIAL FUNNEL - IMPLEMENTATION SUMMARY
========================================

What was built:
--------------
5-stage automated email + WhatsApp funnel for trial users.


Files Created/Modified:
-----------------------

1. src/lib/email-templates.ts
   - trialFunnelWelcome()    → Day 0: Welcome + first steps
   - trialFunnelDay3()       → Day 3: Progress check + tips
   - trialFunnelDay7()       → Day 7: Halfway + what you'll lose
   - trialFunnelDay12()      → Day 12: Urgent - data at risk
   - trialFunnelDay14()      → Day 14: Trial ended - restricted

2. src/lib/whatsapp.ts
   - formatTrialWelcome()    → WhatsApp welcome message
   - formatTrialDay3()       → Personalized by case count
   - formatTrialDay7()       → Urgency message
   - formatTrialDay12()      → Red alert
   - formatTrialDay14()      → Final reminder

3. src/app/api/subscriptions/trial-funnel/route.ts
   - GET  → Cron trigger (runs daily)
   - POST → Manual admin trigger
   - Deduplicates via whatsapp_logs
   - Personalizes by user's case count

4. vercel.json
   - Cron: /api/subscriptions/trial-funnel (9 AM UTC daily)
   - Cron: /api/reminders/cron (6 PM UTC daily)


Trial Funnel Flow:
------------------

Day 0  ──→  Welcome email + WhatsApp
           "Your trial starts! Here's how to begin"

Day 3  ──→  Progress check
           "You created X cases. Keep going!"
           (If 0 cases: "Create your first case")

Day 7  ──→  Halfway warning
           "7 days left. Here's what you'll lose:
            ❌ WhatsApp reminders
            ❌ AI research
            ❌ X cases locked"

Day 12 ──→  Urgent alert
           "⚠️ 2 DAYS LEFT! Your data is at risk"

Day 14 ──→  Trial ended
           "Your account is restricted. Subscribe to continue"
